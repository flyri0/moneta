# Reports

**Reports** opens on an overview: a card per report with this month's picture. Tap a card for the
full report, with a chart, a table and a **period** to choose: this month, last month, the last 3,
6 or 12 months, this year, all time, or a custom range.

![The reports overview: spending by category, net worth, cash flow and spending by payee.](img/reports-light.png)

Spending, cash flow and trends read your budget accounts; transfers between your own accounts are
neither spending nor income. Net worth and the accounts report include tracking accounts too.

## The reports {#list}

- **Spending by category**: where the money went, by category or by group, with each one's share.
  Tap a row to see its transactions.
- **Spending by payee**: the same, by who you paid.
- **Net worth**: everything you own minus everything you owe, at the end of each month, across all
  your accounts, tracking ones included. Each account counts as an asset or a debt by the sign of
  its balance.
- **Cash flow**: income against expenses per month, what was left (**Net**), and the **savings
  rate**: the share of income you didn't spend.
- **Spending trends**: how your biggest categories changed month by month, with each one's average.
- **Assets and debts by account**: what each open account holds or owes, and for debts, how much has
  been paid off since their peak.
- **Age of Money**: see below.

## Age of Money {#age-of-money}

Age of Money is how long your money sat in your accounts before you spent it. A higher number
means you are spending money you earned longer ago: living less paycheck to paycheck.

Moneta follows the money through your checking, savings and cash accounts. Every inflow is a pile
dated the day it arrived. Each payment out takes money from the oldest pile first, and its age is
how long that money waited. The figure is the average age over your **last 10 payments**, worked
out for the end of each month.

- It appears once there are 10 payments out of those accounts.
- Card purchases count when you pay the card, which is when money actually leaves.
- Money spent that never came in (an overdraft) counts as zero days old, and the next inflows pay
  it back before they can be spent.

## Customizing the overview {#customize}

**Customize** lets you reorder the cards (drag them, or use the arrows) and switch off the ones you
don't use. Hidden reports stay listed at the bottom, and **Restore default** brings back the
original layout.
