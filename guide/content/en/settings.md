# Settings

**Settings** gathers what applies to the whole app and to the open budget. Backups, storage and
budget files have their own page: [Your data and backups](data.md).

## Appearance and language {#appearance}

Under **Customization**:

- **Theme**: **System** follows your device's light or dark mode; **Light** and **Dark** keep one.
- **Accent color**: ten colors for buttons, links and highlights.
- **Language**: English or Portuguese (Brazil). Moneta starts in your browser's language when it
  is one of these.

These choices belong to this device and browser: they apply to every budget here, and another
device keeps its own.

## Budget details {#budget-details}

**Budget details** belong to the open budget:

- **Budget name**, as it shows in Budget files.
- **Currency**, which sets how amounts are shown and how many decimal places they have.
- **Number and date format**: the order of day and month, and the separators for thousands and
  decimals. It doesn't have to match the language: an English-speaking app can show amounts the
  Brazilian way, and the other way around.

Once a budget has amounts in it, its currency can only change to one with the same number of
decimal places: amounts are stored as whole units of the smallest coin, so a change from 2 to 0
decimals would change every value.
