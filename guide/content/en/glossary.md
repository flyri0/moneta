# Glossary

The words Moneta uses, in alphabetical order.

### Activity {#activity}

What came in and went out of a category in a month, from your transactions. Spending is negative.

### Assigned {#assigned}

What you gave a category in a month. It comes out of Ready to Assign.

### Available {#available}

What a category still has: what carried over from last month, plus Assigned, plus Activity. Red when it is negative ([overspending](budgeting.md#overspending)).

### Budget account {#budget-account}

An account whose money is budgeted in categories: checking, savings, cash and credit cards. Its transactions need a category. Shown as **On budget**.

### Carryover {#carryover}

What passes from one month to the next in a category. A positive balance always carries over; a negative one only with [Roll overspending over](budgeting.md#carryover).

### Category {#category}

An envelope for money with one purpose: Rent, Groceries, Vacation. Categories sit in groups.

### Cleared {#cleared}

A transaction the bank has already processed: it appears on your statement. Mark it with its checkbox; imported transactions come in cleared.

### Goal {#goal}

What a category aims for: an amount every month, or a balance to save up, optionally by a month ([Goals](budgeting.md#goals)).

### Import rule {#import-rule}

A rule that gives a payee (and a category) to statement lines whose description matches it ([Payee rules](payees.md#payee-rules)).

### Installments {#installments}

A card purchase paid in equal parts, one a month, each entered on its date ([Installments](accounts.md#installments)).

### Overspending {#overspending}

Spending more than a category had. Cover it by moving money, or it comes out of next month's Ready to Assign.

### Payee {#payee}

Who you paid or who paid you.

### Ready to Assign {#ready-to-assign}

Money in your budget accounts that has no job yet. The goal is to bring it to zero ([Ready to Assign](budgeting.md#ready-to-assign)).

### Reconciled {#reconciled}

A transaction checked against the bank's balance on a given day. It stays cleared, and changing it warns you ([Reconcile](accounts.md#reconcile)).

### Schedule {#schedule}

A transaction that repeats, entered by itself or with a tap ([Scheduled transactions](schedules.md)).

### Split {#split}

One transaction divided across several categories ([Splits](transactions.md#splits)).

### Starting balance {#starting-balance}

An account's first transaction: what it held, or owed, when you added it.

### Tracking account {#tracking-account}

An account that counts toward net worth but stays out of the budget: investments, loans, other assets and debts. Its transactions have no category.

### Transfer {#transfer}

Money moving between two of your accounts. Between budget accounts it doesn't change the budget.
